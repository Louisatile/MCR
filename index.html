OUR STORY

An independent carpenter, trusted across Suffolk

Maltings Carpentry & Renovations is an independent, family-run carpentry business based locally in Suffolk. We've spent over 12 years building a reputation on precision, care and honest craftsmanship, the kind that's meant to last.

As a local, independent business, we bring the same attention to detail to every job, whether it's a fitted wardrobe or a full renovation. We proudly cover Suffolk including Ipswich, Saxmundham, Woodbridge, Aldeburgh and Framlingham, extending out to Stowmarket and Bury St Edmunds.
