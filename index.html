OUR STORY

An independent carpenter, trusted across Suffolk

Maltings Carpentry & Renovations is an independent carpentry business based locally in Suffolk. With over 15 years' experience in the building industry, we bring precision, care and honest craftsmanship to every job.

As a local, independent business, we bring the same attention to detail to every job, big or small, whether that's a fitted wardrobe or a full renovation. We cover Suffolk including Ipswich, Saxmundham, Woodbridge, Aldeburgh and Framlingham, and surrounding areas.
