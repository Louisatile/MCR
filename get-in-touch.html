FAQS

Common questions

What areas of Suffolk do you cover?
We're based in Snape and cover Suffolk including Aldeburgh, Saxmundham, Woodbridge, Framlingham, Ipswich, Stowmarket and Bury St Edmunds. If you're further afield, get in touch anyway, depending on the size and location of the job, we're often able to make it work.
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes, every quote is free and no-obligation. Tell us about your project using the form above and we'll come back to you with honest advice on what's possible. For a rough idea of pricing beforehand, see how much a carpenter costs.
What's the process after I submit an enquiry?
Once you submit an enquiry, we'll review the details and get back to you within 2 working days. From there, we can usually give you an initial quote, and if you're happy to go ahead, we'll work with you to arrange a date to get the job booked in.
How long until work can begin? Is there a waiting list?
This depends on the size of the job, the time of year and how full the diary is when you enquire. Once we've quoted and you're ready to proceed, we'll agree a start date together, get in touch and we can give you a realistic timeframe for your project.
What are your payment terms?
We invoice on completion of the work, with a 14-day period to pay. For larger jobs, payment terms can be discussed and negotiated to suit the size and stages of the project.
