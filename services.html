WHAT WE DO

Services we offer

From a single fitted wardrobe to a full renovation, we bring the same attention to detail to every job across Suffolk. Tap a service below for more detail.

Bespoke Joinery & Fitted Furniture

Fitted wardrobes, alcove cabinets, built-in storage, media units and bookcases.

Every piece is measured on site and made to order, so there's no wasted space and no compromise on fit or finish, even in awkward alcoves or sloped ceilings. We also handle general carpentry repairs and snagging lists, no job too small.

Enquire about your fitted furniture

Kitchens

Kitchen fitting and installation, bespoke cabinetry and worktops built to last.

We handle the carpentry side of your kitchen project start to finish: installing base and wall units, fitting worktops, plinths and end panels, making sure everything sits square and level, even in older properties where walls and floors rarely are.

Enquire about your new kitchen

Staircases

Repairs, replacements, balustrades and handrails, fitted with precision.

Whether it's a creaking step that needs sorting, a full replacement, or a new balustrade to modernise the look, staircases carry weight and safety concerns that need proper joinery, not a quick fix.

Enquire about your staircase

Doors, Windows & Flooring

Internal and external doors, sash window restoration, plus hardwood floor fitting and repairs.

Door frames, skirting and architrave fitted properly, plus sash window repair and restoration for period properties. We finish flooring work with matching skirting boards so the whole room ties together.

Enquire about your doors or windows

Renovations & Extensions

Period property renovation and listed building carpentry, plus extensions across Suffolk.

Roof timbers, loft conversions and stud walls for extensions. We're comfortable working alongside builders and other trades on larger projects, and equally happy taking on the carpentry package for a full renovation on our own.

Enquire about your renovation

Garden & Outdoor

Decking, pergolas, sheds, fencing and gates built for the outdoors.

Built using timber and fixings suited to the exposure your garden actually gets. Good outdoor carpentry is as much about detailing against water and movement as it is about the finished look.

Enquire about your garden project

Flat-pack & pre-packed furniture assembly

Picked up a flat-pack wardrobe or IKEA-style furniture and would rather it was put together properly? We assemble and install pre-packed furniture too, and can build it in to look genuinely fitted rather than freestanding, with proper fixing, levelling and finishing touches around it.
