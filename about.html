About Maltings Carpentry & Renovations

I'm Will, the carpenter behind Maltings Carpentry & Renovations. I've worked in the building industry for 15+ years, right here in Suffolk.

I didn't start out as a carpenter. I began as a bricklayer, then moved into window fitting, before settling into carpentry, picking up hands on experience across pretty much every trade a house needs along the way. That grounding means I understand how a build actually goes together, not just the joinery.

These days I run Maltings Carpentry & Renovations as an independent business, covering Suffolk and the surrounding areas. I care about getting the details right and about a job that actually looks good in the finished house.
