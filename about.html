About Maltings Carpentry & Renovations

I'm Will, the carpenter behind Maltings Carpentry & Renovations. With over 15 years' experience in the building industry, I've built my career working on homes throughout Suffolk and the surrounding areas.

My background covers more than just carpentry. I started out as a bricklayer before moving into window fitting and eventually specialising in carpentry and renovations. This has given me broad, practical experience across a range of building trades and a thorough understanding of how different elements of a project work together.

Today, I run Maltings Carpentry & Renovations as an independent business, providing high-quality carpentry and renovation work across Suffolk. I take a straightforward approach to every project, with a focus on quality workmanship, attention to detail and a professional finish.

Whether you already have a clear plan in mind or simply have an idea you'd like to explore, I'm always happy to have a conversation. Get in touch to discuss your project, talk through your ideas and work out how to turn them into a practical plan and, ultimately, something you can see and enjoy in your home.
