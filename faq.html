Frequently Asked Questions

Looking for a reliable carpenter or joiner in Suffolk? Whether you're planning bespoke fitted furniture, a kitchen renovation, new flooring, a loft conversion or a larger home renovation, here are the answers to the questions we're asked most. General Carpentry

What areas does Maltings Carpentry & Renovations cover?
We're based in Snape, Suffolk, and work with homeowners across the surrounding area, including Aldeburgh, Saxmundham, Woodbridge, Framlingham, Ipswich and Bury St Edmunds. If you're located elsewhere in Suffolk, it's still worth getting in touch – depending on the project, we may be able to help.
What type of carpentry work do you do?
We provide a wide range of carpentry and joinery services, from smaller jobs and repairs through to larger renovation projects, including bespoke fitted furniture, fitted wardrobes, alcove cabinets, media units, bookcases, doors, flooring, kitchens, bathrooms, roof timbers, stud walls, exterior carpentry and more. If your project involves timber, joinery or making better use of the space in your home, get in touch to discuss your requirements.
Do you work on small carpentry jobs?
Yes. Not every project needs to be a large renovation – we can help with individual carpentry jobs, repairs, alterations, finishing work and snagging lists where appropriate. If you're unsure whether your job is something we can take on, simply get in touch with the details and we'll let you know.
Do you provide bespoke carpentry?
Yes, bespoke carpentry and joinery is a key part of what we do. Fitted wardrobes, alcove storage, bookcases, media units, cabinetry and other built-in furniture can be designed around your room, your storage requirements and the look you want to achieve, rather than simply fitting an off-the-shelf solution.

Bespoke Joinery & Fitted Furniture

Can you build fitted wardrobes in Suffolk?
Yes. We design and build fitted wardrobes to make the most of the available space in your bedroom – whether that's full-height wardrobes, built-in storage around awkward spaces, or a combination of cupboards, drawers and shelving.
Can you make bespoke alcove cabinets and shelving?
Yes. Bespoke alcove cabinets, shelving and built-in storage are ideal for making use of spaces that standard furniture doesn't fit properly, including around chimney breasts and other awkward areas, tailored to your room and preferred finish.
Can you build a bespoke media wall?
Yes. Bespoke media walls and entertainment units can be designed around your television, storage requirements and the proportions of your room, creating a practical built-in feature that suits your home.
Do you make bespoke bookcases and built-in storage?
Yes. Bespoke bookcases, shelving and fitted storage can be designed to suit both the space available and the way you want to use it – particularly useful where standard furniture leaves gaps or doesn't make efficient use of the room.

Kitchens

Do you offer kitchen fitting in Suffolk?
Yes. We provide kitchen fitting and installation, including bespoke cabinetry and worktops. If you're replacing your kitchen or renovating an existing space, get in touch to discuss the carpentry and fitting requirements.
Can you help with a kitchen renovation?
Yes. We work on kitchen projects as part of wider home renovations as well as individual kitchen fitting work. If you're considering changing the layout, replacing cabinetry or improving the functionality of your kitchen, get in touch to discuss your plans.
Can you alter existing kitchen cabinets?
Depending on the job, existing cabinetry can sometimes be altered, repaired or adapted rather than completely replaced. The best approach depends on the condition of the existing units, the changes you want to make and the overall design of the kitchen.

Bathrooms

Do you carry out bathroom carpentry?
Yes. Our bathroom carpentry services include bespoke vanity units, bathroom panelling and fitted storage – particularly useful for making the most of limited space while keeping the finished design clean and practical.
Can you build bespoke bathroom storage?
Yes. We can create fitted storage and cabinetry designed around the available space and your requirements, making better use of awkward areas while keeping everyday bathroom items organised and out of sight.

Doors, Flooring & Joinery

Do you fit internal and external doors?
Yes. We work with both internal and external doors, including fitting and associated carpentry work. If you have an existing door that needs replacing, altering or rehanging, get in touch with the details of the job.
Do you repair or restore sash windows?
Yes, sash window restoration is one of the services we offer. If you have traditional or period windows that require carpentry attention, we can discuss the condition of the windows and what you're hoping to achieve.
Do you fit wooden flooring?
Yes. We provide hardwood floor fitting and repairs. If you're installing new flooring as part of a renovation or need repairs to an existing timber floor, we can discuss the most suitable approach for your property.

Renovations & Extensions

Do you work on home renovations?
Yes. We undertake carpentry as part of home renovation projects, from individual rooms through to larger property improvements, including period property carpentry, listed building carpentry, stud walls, roof timbers and other timber-related work.
Do you work on period properties?
Yes, we have experience working on period property renovation projects. Older properties often require a more considered approach because existing construction, materials and detailing can differ significantly from modern buildings.
Do you carry out carpentry work on listed buildings?
Yes, we offer listed building carpentry. If you're planning work on a listed property, it's important that any necessary permissions and project requirements are understood before work begins – we can advise on the carpentry element of your project.
Can you help with an extension?
Yes. We undertake carpentry work associated with extensions, including roof timbers and stud walls. If you're planning a new extension in Suffolk, get in touch early so we can discuss the carpentry requirements and how they fit into the wider project.
Do you work on loft conversions?
Yes, loft conversion carpentry is one of the areas we cover, which can include timber work, stud walls and other carpentry required to transform the loft space.

Roofing & Exterior Carpentry

Do you carry out roof timber work?
Yes. We provide roof timber carpentry as part of renovation, extension and other building projects. If you have damaged, altered or new roof timber requirements, get in touch with details of the project.
Do you repair fascias and soffits?
Yes. Fascias, soffits and other exterior carpentry are among the services we offer. If exterior timberwork around your property is damaged, deteriorating or needs replacing as part of a wider project, get in touch.
Do you work on guttering?
Yes, guttering forms part of our roofing and exterior services. For larger roofing or exterior projects, we can discuss the overall requirements and determine what carpentry work is involved.

Getting a Quote

How do I get a quote for carpentry work?
The easiest way is to get in touch and tell us about your project. It helps to include as much information as possible, such as your location, the type of work you need, the approximate size of the project, any particular materials or finishes you're considering, your preferred timescale, and photos of the existing space, if relevant. For larger projects, we can discuss your ideas and requirements in more detail before providing a quote.
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes. We offer free quotes for carpentry and renovation projects. We'll discuss what you want to achieve, consider the practical requirements and provide advice where appropriate.
Can you help me decide what would work best?
Absolutely. You don't necessarily need to have everything designed before contacting us. Part of the benefit of working with an independent carpenter is being able to discuss the practical details, proportions, materials, finishes and different ways of approaching a project. If you have an idea but aren't sure how to make it work, get in touch and we can talk it through.
How far in advance should I book a carpenter?
This depends on the size and complexity of the project and our current workload. For larger renovations, extensions and bespoke joinery projects, it's worth getting in touch as early as possible so we can discuss your requirements and potential timescales. For smaller carpentry jobs, availability may be more flexible.
