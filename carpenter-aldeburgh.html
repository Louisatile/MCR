LOCAL WORK

Carpentry on the Suffolk coast

Aldeburgh's mix of fishermen's cottages, Victorian seafront houses and holiday lets brings its own challenges. Salt air is hard on timber and fixings, cottages are compact, and many properties sit within the conservation area.

We're based a short drive inland in Snape and also cover Thorpeness, Aldringham, Leiston and Knodishall, as well as nearby Saxmundham. For second homes and holiday lets, we're happy to work around booking calendars and keyholders.

Thinking about storage for a compact cottage? Our guide to IKEA vs bespoke fitted wardrobes covers the options, and you can see a media wall we built in Aldeburgh.

Planning an outdoor project? See the hardwood decking we built in Ipswich.

Typical work in and around Aldeburgh

  • External doors, windows and joinery chosen to cope with coastal weather
  • Fitted storage that makes the most of compact cottage rooms
  • Refreshing holiday lets between bookings
  • Repairs and like-for-like replacements on older and listed properties
  • Decking and garden joinery built with suitable timber and fixings

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Do you work on holiday lets and second homes in Aldeburgh?

Yes, a good proportion of our work here is for owners of holiday lets and second homes, we're happy to work around booking calendars and keyholders.

Does coastal weather affect the carpentry you'd recommend?

It can. Salt air is harder on timber and standard fixings, so we'll talk through suitable materials and treatments for anything exposed to the elements, rather than just fitting the cheapest option.

Can you work on a listed or conservation area property in Aldeburgh?

Yes, we regularly work on older properties in and around Aldeburgh's conservation area, and we're happy to discuss what's appropriate before starting.

How much does a carpenter cost in Aldeburgh?

Pricing depends on the job, but as a guide most independent Suffolk carpenters charge around £200 to £300 a day. We'll give you a clear quote up front, with a site visit for bigger projects. See our full guide to carpentry costs.

Do you cover villages near Aldeburgh as well?

Yes, we cover the surrounding area including Thorpeness, Snape and the villages further inland, get in touch with your location and we'll confirm.

We also regularly work in Saxmundham, Woodbridge, Ipswich and Framlingham, as well as throughout the rest of Suffolk.
